Selected work

Cedar Ridge House

A 1,400-square-foot house for a family who wanted fewer rooms and more porch. The gable form sheds snow and keeps the budget in check; a long covered porch does most of the living.

Foundry Lofts

Adaptive reuse of a 1920s ironworks building into six residential lofts. We kept the sawtooth roof and the steel window grid, and cut a new courtyard through the middle for light.

How we work

The same four phases, whether it's a writing studio or a six-unit building.

01

Site & story

We start by walking the site and listening to how you actually want to live, not how a magazine says you should.

02

Form & light

Early massing studies test how a building sits on its site and where the light falls through the day.

03

Material & detail

We choose materials that age well and detail the places where they meet, since that's where a building either holds up or doesn't.

04

Construction & care

We stay involved through construction, and check back for years after, because a building keeps changing after it's built.
